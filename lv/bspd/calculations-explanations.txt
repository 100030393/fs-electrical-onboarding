Here's a quick explanation of how I got to some of the numbers in my BSPD circuit!! 

1. The threshold voltages:
	1. HASS Current Sensor: 108V is the nominal battery voltage, and according to the FSAE manual, "Tractive system current is at a level where 5 kW of electrical power in the DCcircuit is delivered at nominal voltage”. So... 5kw is the power we're aiming for!! Using P = IV, I found that the current we want to convert into an equivalent voltage for the sensor is 46.29A. The HASS-50 datasheet gives the output voltage as 2.5 +- 0.625(primary current/nominal primary current). Then we look at the lower boundary, although in my sketch I made a mistake and took the upper boundary. Subbing the values in, I found that the threshold voltage is 1.921 V. Using the equation Vout = Vin(R2/(Rin+R2)), I found the values for the resistors I used for the voltage divider. *Keep in mind that this assumes that we're hooked up to 5V power supply
	2. Brake Pressure Sensor: I was told that the voltage threshold for this would match 200-300 psi. I took the middle value(250 psi) as reference. As per the datasheet(found here:https://aimsportsystems.com.au/download/doc/eng/sensors/pressure/Datasheet_Pressure0-2000PSI_100_eng.pdf), voltage outputs range from 0.5V/0psi to 4.5V/2000psi. To find where 250 lands in these numbers, I got some help from this website(https://calculator.academy/pressure-to-voltage-calculator/). It's around 1V!!! For the voltage divider values I used the same process as the HASS sensor. :)))

2. 555 timer stuff: I had an easier time with finding the capcitor and resistor values for the 555 timer! I used this equation: t=1.1RC. To get to a time of 0.1 seconds, I found that a combo of a 10K resistor and a 9.1uF capcitor could work. Ofc, these numbers can be tweaked (so long as we end up with 0.1 sec) but this is just what I wanted to use!
